The meaning behind THAW

Named for the moment a season turns.

Inspired by the transition from winter to spring, THAW represents renewed energy, opportunity, and growth. Our name reflects our belief that slower periods do not mean standing still. By combining fresh perspectives with practical business strategies, we aim to help businesses build momentum even when conditions are challenging.

Our forest green and warm orange branding symbolizes growth, optimism, and new beginnings.

Our team and company biography

THAW Strategy Consulting is a four-person marketing and business strategy team based in Edmonton. We look for opportunity inside difficult market conditions: the seasonal slowdown, the quiet weekday afternoon, the customer whose habits have changed.

We combine market research, consumer insights, and creative problem-solving to develop targeted marketing initiatives, customer engagement strategies, and practical business solutions. We focus on realistic, measurable recommendations that align with a client's objectives and resources.

At THAW, we believe effective consulting goes beyond identifying problems. It means turning meaningful insights into actionable strategies that help businesses adapt, build momentum, and achieve lasting success.

How we work

Nothing is proposed until it is understood.

Our approach begins with listening and research. We seek to understand a client's goals, target audiences, operating environment, and constraints before proposing solutions.

01 · Listen

Conversations with the people who live with the problem: business owners, residents, and visitors.

02 · Research

Primary and secondary research into customer behaviour, market conditions, and the barriers that keep people away.

03 · Evaluate

Every option is tested for relevance, practicality, and measurable value, including what it would take to deliver.

04–05 · Recommend and measure

Clear, coordinated recommendations a business can act on, with the indicators that show whether they are working.

RFP response · Old Strathcona Business Association

A district whose year is decided in its quietest hours.

Old Strathcona is one of Edmonton's most established commercial, cultural, and entertainment districts, and the Old Strathcona Business Association represents nearly 600 businesses within it. Its request for proposal, The Future of Whyte Ave, asks for an evidence-based strategy that keeps the district vital through the whole day and the whole year.

That is the kind of problem THAW was named for. Read how we interpret the brief, what we already see as neighbours of the street, and the questions we will bring to each season.
