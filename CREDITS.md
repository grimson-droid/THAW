## Artificial intelligence use

The web page format of the biographies was coded with the assistance of Claude (Anthropic), used under the course's Level 3 AI usage permission and the BUS 202 team's written confirmation (email of October 13, 2026) that AI may be used to code the website. All biographies, the company biography, the brand name, logo, colour palette, and the "What we accomplish" and "The meaning behind THAW" text were written and designed by Group 87 and are reproduced from `Group 87_Biographies_1DOCUMENT`. Claude wrote the remaining page copy under the group's direction, based on that document and the OSBA RFP.
